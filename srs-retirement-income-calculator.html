What is the SRS?

The Supplementary Retirement Scheme (SRS) is a voluntary retirement account that sits alongside CPF. Contributions earn tax relief in the year you contribute, and the money can be invested. The rules reward leaving the money until retirement and drawing it down gradually.

How this calculation works

Your balance keeps earning the assumed return while level monthly withdrawals bring it to zero by the end of the period. Each withdrawal is split 50% non-taxable and 50% taxable. The tax rate applies only to the taxable half, so the effective tax on the whole withdrawal is half the tax rate.

PMT = (r × PV) ÷ [1 − (1 + r)^(−n)] PV = starting SRS balance r = annual return ÷ 12 (as a decimal) n = years × 12 payments
