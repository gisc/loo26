This estimator finds a single level monthly payment that uses up the whole portfolio by the end of the period, while the remaining balance keeps earning the assumed return.

PMT = (r × PV) ÷ [1 − (1 + r)^(−n)]

Important assumptions

  • A constant annual investment return.
  • Monthly return = annual return ÷ 12.
  • Monthly withdrawals stay level throughout.
  • Returns are smooth, with no ups and downs.
  • No investment volatility is modelled.
  • No sequence-of-returns risk is modelled.
  • No inflation adjustment.
  • No fees or taxes.
  • No extra top-ups or one-off withdrawals.
  • The portfolio is run down to about S$0 at the end.
1M65 Insight: Plan your lifestyle on the lower return scenarios. Treat anything above that as a bonus, not a budget.
