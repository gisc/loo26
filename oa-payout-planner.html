What is an annuity?

An annuity turns a lump sum into a regular pay cheque. Your OA keeps earning interest while you draw from it, so the money lasts exactly the years you picked and reaches $0 right on schedule. Think of it as paying yourself a salary in retirement.

Want income for life? That's what CPF LIFE (from your Retirement Account) is for. This planner is about your OA.

How this works

  • Your monthly amount is worked out so that your balance, plus the interest it keeps earning, is shared out evenly over the years you chose.
  • Uses the guaranteed 2.5% a year OA rate. CPF's extra interest (up to 2% more on your first $60,000) is not included, so real results may be slightly better.
  • Assumes you draw at the end of each month. Actual OA withdrawal rules depend on your age and the amount set aside in your Retirement Account. Check cpf.gov.sg for your own situation.
